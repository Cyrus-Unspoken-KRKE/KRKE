A declared rule generates a possible portrayal. The graph retains the narrated time and the later narrator’s viewpoint.

“Ideal ruler” is a narrow teaching label for selected care, mercy, relief and reward cues. Three passages also demonstrate a prior condition or possible human or political impact. These are interpretations of sources. The input texts are paraphrases, so this is a bounded demonstration.
